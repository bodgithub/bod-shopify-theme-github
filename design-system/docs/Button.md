Pill button. The theme's pill button. `primary` is the blue gradient CTA (add-to-cart, hero CTAs); `outline` is the periwinkle informational pill; `white` sits on navy/gradient surfaces.

```tsx
<Button>Add to Cart</Button>
<Button variant="outline" size="medium">Learn More</Button>
```
Use `fullWidth` for mobile add-to-cart rows.
