Info pill trigger. Periwinkle pill button with a circled "+" that opens the standard clinical Lightbox when `popupTitle`/`popupContent` are set. Used under stat cards ("What are HOMA-IR Levels +").

```tsx
<InfoButton label="What is the 40:1 Ratio" popupTitle="The 40:1 Ratio" popupContent={<p>...</p>} />
```
