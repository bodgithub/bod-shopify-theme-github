## Option 1 — The plain answer
**Use for:** product page FAQ, DMs, comment replies

Inositol is for women whose symptoms all trace back to one thing: how their body handles insulin.

- It supports insulin signalling. That's the whole mechanism. Everything else on this list is downstream of it.
- It's for cycles that show up whenever they feel like it. 40 days, 60 days, twice in a month, or not at all.
- It's for the sugar cravings that aren't about willpower. The 3pm crash where you'd eat the whole pantry. (This is the one people feel first — usually inside 2–4 weeks.)
- It's for hormonal acne along the jaw and chin, the kind that ignores every skincare routine you've thrown at it.
- It's for the PMS week that takes over your personality. Mood, irritability, the 5 days you'd rather skip.
- It's for coming off the pill and waiting for your body to remember what it's supposed to do.
- It's for women with a PCOS diagnosis and no plan. You got told "lose weight" and "come back when you want kids." This is the thing that wasn't offered.
- It's for women with no diagnosis at all who recognise every symptom on this list. (Huge chunk of your audience. Don't gate the answer behind a diagnosis.)

**What it's not for:** a quick fix. It's a daily 4.1g dose — two scoops, one serving — and most people notice cravings and energy first, cycle changes around month 2–3.

## Option 2 — Symptom-first, no mechanism
**Use for:** TikTok / short-form, cold audience

Lead with recognition, not education.

> Your period comes when it wants.
> You crash at 3pm and it's not because you're tired.
> Your skin breaks out on your jaw, always the same spot.
> You're exhausted after eight hours of sleep.
> PMS eats a full week of your month.
> You came off birth control and your body never came back online.
>
> These aren't six separate problems. They're one problem showing up in six places. That problem is how your body responds to insulin — and inositol is the thing your body already uses to run that signal properly.

(Six lines, one twist. That structure holds attention better than an explainer because the payoff is a reframe, not a definition.)
